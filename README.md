## How It Works

1. **Pick your hunt** — Choose the indoor romantic dinner hunt or the outdoor adventure hunt (or do both on different dates)
2. **Read the planning guide** — Follow the 2-week timeline so nothing feels rushed
3. **Customize the clues** — Every clue has tips for personalizing it to your relationship
4. **Choose your love notes** — Select from 30 templates and fill in the personal details
5. **Set up and enjoy** — The guide walks you through setup so the only thing you have to do on Valentine's Day is show up
