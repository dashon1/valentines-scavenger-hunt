# Adventure Date Hunt
## 10-Clue Outdoor Scavenger Hunt Through Meaningful Locations

This hunt takes place across multiple locations in your city, neighborhood, or meaningful outdoor spaces. It's designed to take 1.5–2.5 hours depending on how many activities you add and how close your locations are to each other.

The structure: 10 clues that move through increasingly meaningful locations, building toward a final reveal at a place with emotional significance — the coffee shop where you first met, a park with a memory, a rooftop, or home with something special set up for when you arrive.

**Important:** Read through this entire document before your planning session. You will need to map your own locations to each clue. The adventure hunt is more personal than the indoor hunt precisely because the locations must be specific to your relationship.

---

## Before You Start: Map Your Route

Go through the 10 clue "archetypes" below and assign a real location to each one. You need:

1. A starting location (where you hand them the first clue)
2. 8 intermediate stops (mix of relationship-meaningful and fun)
3. A final destination with a meaningful reveal

Write your location list on paper first. Then map the route — order the stops so the travel between them is logical, not zigzagging all over the city.

**Clue weatherproofing:** Put each clue in a small zip-lock bag before hiding it. Tape it to a signpost, park bench, building exterior, or tree at eye level. A clue blowing away mid-hunt ruins the mood.

---

## Clue 1 — The Starting Location
### (Delivered by hand — this is not hidden)

**The clue:**
> "Today, we're going on an adventure. Not a big adventure — the kind that's made of small things: places we've been together, places that mean something to one of us, and a few surprises I've been planning longer than you know.
>
> Every clue will take you somewhere. At each stop, there's a small moment waiting — something to read, something to do, something to remember.
>
> Your first real stop: [LOCATION 1 — fill in a place that started something for you both, OR a place that's simply "where we began today," like your front door, your car, a coffee shop you go to on Sunday mornings]."

**Tip for Location 1:** The first stop sets the tone. Make it accessible — don't start with a 20-minute drive to an obscure location. Start close to home or where you're beginning the day.

---

## Clue 2 — A Place You Go Together Regularly
**Archetype:** A place that's become "yours" — a diner, a coffee shop, a park trail, a neighborhood bench

**The clue:**
> "This is one of our places. Not because it's special to anyone else — because we decided it was. The best places aren't famous ones. They're the ones that got good because we kept showing up.
>
> Take a minute here. Look around like it's the first time. [Optional activity — order one thing, sit for a few minutes, or just stand here together for one minute without checking your phone.]
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 2 — fill in a place meaningful from early in your relationship, a first date spot, or a location with a specific memory]."

**Activity:** Leave a note at this location that references something specific that happened here. "Remember the time we [specific memory at this spot]? I think about that more than you know."

**Tip:** This clue works best if you can arrange with a staff member to hand them something when they arrive — a small envelope from "the person who planned this" or even just the next clue passed through a barista.

---

## Clue 3 — A First: First Date / First Time You Said Something Important
**Archetype:** The location of a significant early moment — a first date restaurant, a bar where you had a deep conversation, a park where you first held hands

**The clue:**
> "This place exists in a specific chapter of our story. The chapter where I started paying attention differently. Where I thought: this person. This one.
>
> I won't embarrass either of us by listing every detail I remember about [this night / this afternoon / this specific conversation] — but I remember a lot more than you might think.
>
> [Add 1–2 specific lines about what you remember from this location — something they said, something you noticed, a detail from that day/night]
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 3 — somewhere connected to something you share: a hobby, a neighborhood you walked through once, a place one of you introduced to the other]."

**Activity:** If possible, recreate one small element from that first visit — the same drink you had, a photo from that time if you have one, a specific line of dialogue you remember.

**Tip:** This clue will hit the hardest. Give it the most personal writing of any clue in the hunt.

---

## Clue 4 — Something One of You Introduced to the Other
**Archetype:** A place, activity, or spot connected to a part of one person's life that the other person got adopted into — a restaurant from their culture, a park from their childhood neighborhood, a shop they loved before you met

**The clue:**
> "I didn't know about this [place / thing / part of the world] until you. Now I can't imagine not knowing it. That's one of my favorite things about being with you — you kept expanding what I thought I liked, what I thought I knew, and what I thought I needed.
>
> [Add a line specific to what this place represents — e.g., "You ordered for me that first time because I had no idea what any of it was. Now it's one of my favorites."]
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 4 — a place connected to something you're working toward together, a neighborhood you talked about moving to, a place you've said "we should come back here"]."

**Activity:** Leave a small gift or purchase something from this location as a symbol — a drink they introduced you to, a small item from a shop they love.

---

## Clue 5 — A Future You've Talked About
**Archetype:** A place that represents something you've talked about doing together — a neighborhood you discussed, a place you've said "we should go back," a park near a house you drove past and said "I'd like to live there someday"

**The clue:**
> "We've stood in this area — or talked about it — and pointed at something in the future. "Someday" and "maybe" and "one day" conversations are some of my favorite kind. Not because I'm in a hurry. Because having a future to talk about means we're both planning to be in it together.
>
> [Add a specific future-focused line — e.g., "You said once that you wanted to live somewhere with mornings like this. I'm working on it."]
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 5 — somewhere that's just beautiful, or fun, or that you've been wanting to take them to for no particular reason except that you think they'd love it]."

**Activity:** At this location, leave a note that says: "One day this. More of this. All of this."

---

## Clue 6 — A Place That's Just Beautiful (No Reason Required)
**Archetype:** A park, a viewpoint, a waterfront, a quiet street, a garden — somewhere that's simply beautiful and that you want to share with them

**The clue:**
> "This spot has no story in our relationship yet. I just wanted you to see it. Sometimes that's enough — finding something good and thinking, 'they'd like this.' You'd like this. I know you well enough to know that.
>
> Stay here for a few minutes. [Optional: there's a note/photo/small gift waiting at [specific spot — a bench, a fountain, a railing] — go find it.]
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 6 — somewhere that represents something you've been through together: a place near a tough time, a hospital where something important happened, a place near a city you moved from or to together]."

**Activity:** Hide the next clue somewhere slightly off the beaten path within this location — a park bench, behind a rock, on a signpost — so there's a small discovery moment within the discovery moment.

---

## Clue 7 — Something You've Been Through Together
**Archetype:** A place connected to a challenge, change, or transition you navigated together — near a hospital, a neighborhood from a harder chapter, a street near an old apartment, a spot near a job you left

**The clue:**
> "Not all of our best chapters have been the easy ones. Some of the ones that built us most were the ones that were honestly kind of terrible at the time. You know which ones I mean.
>
> I'm glad we went through those together. I wouldn't change any of it. Not because I'd choose the hard parts again — but because I'd choose you in the hard parts again, every time.
>
> [Add 1–2 personal lines about what you went through near this location or what it represents — not heavy, but honest]
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 7 — somewhere you've laughed together, a place associated with a funny memory, something lighter after the heavier stop]."

**Activity:** Keep this stop brief — it's the emotional weight stop. The activity is the reflection, not a game. Just be present here for a moment.

---

## Clue 8 — A Funny or Light Memory
**Archetype:** A place associated with something that still makes you both laugh — a restaurant where something ridiculous happened, a spot where you got lost, a place from a trip gone slightly sideways

**The clue:**
> "Okay. After that last stop, we need this one.
>
> [Reference the specific funny memory associated with this location — the more specific, the better. "The night we [specific ridiculous story]" or "The place where we learned that [specific lesson the hard way]."]
>
> I think about this and laugh every single time. You, on the other hand, claim you were totally calm the entire time.
>
> Your next stop: [LOCATION 8 — somewhere that represents something you've built together: your neighborhood, a place near where you live now, a community or group you're part of together]."

**Activity:** Leave something funny here — an inside joke reference, a ridiculous photo of the two of you from that memory, or a fake "historical marker" you made that commemorates the event.

---

## Clue 9 — Where You've Built a Life
**Archetype:** Near your home, your neighborhood, a local spot that's become part of your regular life — the coffee shop near your apartment, the grocery store you always go to together, the park you walk through, the neighborhood that's just yours now

**The clue:**
> "It's easy to look for meaning in big moments — the firsts, the milestones, the trips and the nights that get saved in photos. But I think a lot of what we are is here. In the ordinary version of us. The Tuesday-night version. The grocery-run version. The do-we-want-to-cook-or-just-order version.
>
> This neighborhood, this life, this version of us — I chose it. I'm still choosing it.
>
> One more stop. The one I've been building toward this whole time."

**Activity:** This is the setup clue for the finale. If the finale is back home, make sure everything is set up before they leave on the hunt. If the finale is elsewhere, confirm the location is ready.

---

## Clue 10 — The Final Location
**Found at:** The meaningful final destination you've chosen

This clue is placed at or given at the final destination. The final stop should be one of the following:
- A place with the most significant memory in your relationship (the place you first said "I love you," where you got together, where you got engaged)
- A beautiful destination you've talked about visiting (a rooftop, a favorite restaurant, a place with a view)
- Back home to a romantic setup (dinner, candles, a surprise you've arranged)

**The final clue:**
> "You made it. Ten stops. Ten pieces of us that I wanted to revisit today.
>
> I started planning this because I wanted Valentine's Day to feel like something — not just another card and a dinner reservation, but something that said: I know you. I see what we've built. I think about us, about the details, about all of it.
>
> [Add 3–5 deeply personal lines here — something you love about them, something you're grateful for, something you're looking forward to. This is the part that matters most. Write it last. Don't rush it.]
>
> Happy Valentine's Day. I love you. Now let's [finish the adventure / have dinner / enjoy what I've set up] — you've earned it."

**Final reveal:**
Whatever you've arranged for the finale — dinner, a gift, a picnic, a view, a reservation — should be waiting. If you're outside, have something tangible at this location: a wrapped gift, a card, a blanket laid out, a bottle of wine and glasses already poured.
