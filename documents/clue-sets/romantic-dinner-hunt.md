# Romantic Dinner Hunt
## 10-Clue Indoor Scavenger Hunt Leading to a Candlelit Dinner

This hunt is designed for a home setting and takes 20–35 minutes to complete. The final clue leads to a set dinner table — either a home-cooked meal or a "romantic restaurant at home" setup you've arranged in advance.

Each clue entry includes:
- **The clue** — ready to copy onto a card
- **Where to hide it** — the location the clue points to
- **Romantic activity** — something to set up at that location (optional but recommended for 2–3 stops)
- **Customization tip** — how to make it personal to your relationship

---

## Clue 1 — The Opening Clue
### (Delivered by hand as the hunt begins)

**The clue:**
> "Every great love story has a beginning. Ours started with a moment that felt ordinary and turned out to be everything. Before you find your way to tonight's surprise, find the place in our home where every morning begins — where you wake up slowly and the day hasn't started yet. Your first clue is waiting there."

**Where to hide clue 2:** On their pillow, or on their nightstand, or tucked under a corner of the sheets on their side of the bed.

**Romantic activity at this location:** None needed — this is the starting point. Deliver this clue in person along with a handwritten note that says "I planned something for tonight. Follow the clues."

**Customization tip:** Change "every morning begins" to something more specific — "where you always check your phone first thing" or "where I watch you pretend to be asleep for ten more minutes."

## Clue 2 — The Bedroom
**Found at:** Pillow / nightstand / under the sheets

**The clue:**
> "You are my favorite thing about mornings, even the hard ones. The next place you're going is somewhere you go to pour yourself into something — the place where you fuel up, slow down, and let the day decide what kind of day it will be. Find the thing that makes you feel most like yourself in the morning."

**Where to hide clue 3:** Taped to the coffee maker, hidden inside a mug, tucked behind the espresso machine, or sitting on the kitchen counter near the kettle — wherever their morning drink lives.

**Romantic activity:** Leave their favorite mug out with a note that says "No coffee tonight — there's something better waiting. Keep going."

**Customization tip:** If they don't drink coffee, adapt this to whatever their morning ritual is — their workout clothes drawer, their journal spot, the window seat where they read.

---

## Clue 3 — The Kitchen (Coffee/Drink Station)
**Found at:** Coffee maker / mug / kettle area

**The clue:**
> "You've warmed my heart the way this spot warms your mornings — reliably, completely, and in a way I didn't fully appreciate until I thought about what life looked like before you. Now find somewhere colder. The place where we keep the things that remind us we were thinking of each other — leftovers saved for the other person, the drink they prefer, the snack that's always there 'just in case.' You know where to look."

**Where to hide clue 4:** Taped inside the refrigerator — on their favorite shelf, or attached to their favorite drink, or taped to the inside of the fridge door.

**Romantic activity:** Leave a small piece of their favorite chocolate in the fridge with the clue. A sticky note that says "a snack for the journey."

**Customization tip:** Reference something specific: "the shelf where the [drink they like] always lives" or "behind the leftovers I saved because I knew you'd want some."

## Clue 4 — The Refrigerator
**Found at:** Inside the fridge, attached to their preferred shelf or drink

**The clue:**
> "Cold things don't stay cold when love is nearby. From here, think about the place in our home where time slows down — where we've had the best conversations, stayed up too late, and said things we probably should have said years earlier. The place that's seen us at our most honest."

**Where to hide clue 5:** On the couch — tucked into a cushion, taped to the underside of a throw pillow, or left on their designated seat.

**Romantic activity:** Queue up a playlist on the speaker nearby. A note on the clue: "Sit here for one song before you keep going."

**Customization tip:** "The couch" is the most universal version of this — but if your deepest conversations happen in the car, at the kitchen table, or on the back porch, change the location to match.

---

## Clue 5 — The Couch / Living Room
**Found at:** Tucked into the couch cushions or on their seat

**The clue:**
> "Some of my favorite memories of us happened on this couch. The late nights. The nothing-special evenings that somehow became the ones I think about most. But the next stop isn't about sitting — it's about something we reach for when the day needs to be reset. The place where we step out of who we were at work and remember who we are at home."

**Where to hide clue 6:** In the bathroom — taped to the mirror, on the edge of the tub, attached to the shower door, or left on the counter near their sink essentials.

**Romantic activity:** Leave a single flower or a small candle near the sink. A note: "You always look good. Tonight you look incredible."

**Customization tip:** If they have a specific bathroom ritual you know about (a face routine, a long soak, a skincare step), reference it here: "where you have that whole routine I pretend not to watch."

## Clue 6 — The Bathroom
**Found at:** Mirror, counter, tub edge, or shower door

**The clue:**
> "Clean slate. Fresh start. That's what tonight is — a reminder that we get to keep choosing each other. The next place you're going is somewhere that holds things we've been given, things we've saved, and things we chose to keep. A small collection of who we are together."

**Where to hide clue 7:** Near a bookshelf, photo wall, or the place where they keep meaningful items — framed photos, a trinket shelf, a keepsake box, or their side of the dresser where they keep small meaningful things.

**Romantic activity:** If you have a framed photo of the two of you nearby, tape the clue to the back of the frame and write: "Turn it over."

**Customization tip:** Personalize this for where you actually keep memories — a photo album, a shelf with travel souvenirs, the spot where their grandmother's ring or a gift from you lives.

---

## Clue 7 — The Memory Spot (Bookshelf / Photos / Keepsakes)
**Found at:** Behind a photo frame, on a memory shelf, or inside a keepsake box

**The clue:**
> "Every one of those moments led here — to tonight, to this hunt, to the two of us. But before the finale, there's one more stop. Find the place in our home that connects us to the outside world — the window or door that marks the boundary between out there and in here. Between the day and this."

**Where to hide clue 8:** At the front door, a favorite window, or the back door/balcony entrance. Tuck it in a shoe by the door, tape it to the doorframe at eye level, or leave it on the windowsill.

**Romantic activity:** Leave a single note on the window or door that says: "Whatever happened out there today — it stays out there. Tonight is just us."

**Customization tip:** If they have a specific window they love (a reading nook window, a kitchen window with a good view), use that instead of the front door.

---

## Clue 8 — The Door or Favorite Window
**Found at:** Front door, back door, or windowsill

**The clue:**
> "You came in through that door on the night that changed everything. Or maybe it wasn't that night — maybe it was a hundred ordinary nights that added up to something extraordinary. Either way, you're almost there. The last place before the finale is somewhere you go when the day has been too much and you need something that asks nothing of you in return."

**Where to hide clue 9:** At their favorite chair, a reading nook, their spot by the TV remote, or wherever they retreat when they need to decompress. Leave it tucked under the seat or on the armrest.

**Romantic activity:** Leave a blanket folded neatly on the chair with a note: "One last stop after this, I promise. Then you can stay as long as you want."

**Customization tip:** "The thing that asks nothing of you" could be a book, a show, a playlist, a spot in the sun — adapt to what actually describes their go-to decompression ritual.

---

## Clue 9 — The Favorite Chair / Retreat Spot
**Found at:** Under the seat, on the armrest, or tucked into the cushion of their go-to relaxation spot

**The clue:**
> "You've been on quite a journey tonight — and I've been planning this one for a while. You've visited all the places that hold pieces of our everyday life together. Now it's time for the place where we make something for each other, share something with each other, and sit across from each other and remember that none of this is ordinary at all. Tonight, dinner is waiting. Follow the light."

**Where to hide the final reveal:** The dining table — fully set, with candles lit, flowers if you have them, their favorite drink poured, and dinner ready (or nearly ready). The final clue card should be propped up at their place setting.

---

## Clue 10 — The Final Clue (At the Dinner Table)
**Found at:** Propped at their dinner place setting

**The clue:**
> "You found it. You found me — and I'm so glad you did, every single day.
>
> Tonight is simple: sit down, let me pour you a drink, and let's be here together. No phones, no to-do lists, no plans that don't involve the next few hours. Just us.
>
> I love you. Happy Valentine's Day."

**Romantic activity — the reveal:**
When they arrive at the table, be there. Have their drink poured. Have music playing softly. The candles should already be lit. This is the moment — don't rush it, don't explain it. Just say "I'm glad you're here."

**Customization tip:** Add one deeply personal line to the final clue — something only the two of you would understand. A reference to an inside joke, a memory from a specific date, a thing they said once that stuck with you. The rest of the clue can stay generic. That one line makes the whole hunt worth it.

## Dinner Setup Suggestions

**Option 1 — Cook for them:**
Set the table before the hunt starts. Have the first course (a salad, bread, or appetizer) plated and ready at the table. The main course should be in the oven or on warm hold so it's ready within 10 minutes of them sitting down.

**Option 2 — Restaurant takeout elevated:**
Order from their absolute favorite restaurant (not "good enough" — their actual favorite). Pick it up 30 minutes before the hunt starts. Keep it warm in the oven at 200°F. Set the table as if it were a restaurant: cloth napkins, candles, their favorite drink already poured. Takeout in this context is not a cop-out — it's a considered choice.

**Option 3 — Picnic at the table:**
A charcuterie board, cheeses, grapes, crackers, olives, chocolate — all arranged beautifully on a board. No cooking required. Extremely romantic, surprisingly intimate. Add a bottle of wine and this is the easiest possible dinner setup that still lands perfectly.
