# Love Notes Collection
## 30 Fill-in-the-Blank Templates for Every Tone and Moment

These notes are designed to be used throughout the scavenger hunt — placed at clue locations, tucked into the final reveal, or saved for other moments during Valentine's Day. They cover four styles: funny, heartfelt, memory-based, and future promises. Mix and match — the best gift sequences have variety in tone.

Fill-in sections are marked with [brackets]. Replace them with your specific details before writing or printing.

---

## SECTION 1: Funny Notes
### For when you want them to laugh before they feel things

---

**Note 1 — The Honest Admission**
> I love you despite the fact that you [annoying but endearing habit — e.g., "leave cabinet doors open" / "always fall asleep before the movie ends" / "insist on giving directions even when you're wrong"]. That's how I know it's real.
>
> Happy Valentine's Day. You owe me nothing. (Okay, maybe [their cooking specialty or a favor you want — e.g., "that pasta you make" / "one back rub"].)

---

**Note 2 — The Qualified Compliment**
> Out of everyone I know, you are unquestionably my favorite person to [activity you do together — e.g., "waste time with" / "get lost with" / "order too much food with"]. I didn't know that was a category until you showed up and immediately became the winner.

---

**Note 3 — The Reluctant Romantic**
> I am not normally the type of person who does [gesture you've planned — e.g., "scavenger hunts" / "romantic surprises" / "this much planning"]. I want you to know that I planned all of this while internally pretending I wasn't doing something embarrassingly romantic. For you, I will continue to do embarrassingly romantic things. Don't tell anyone.

---

**Note 4 — The Statistics**
> Fun facts about us:
> - Times I've thought about you today: [a high number]
> - Times I've told you that: [a low number]
> - My ability to describe what you mean to me out loud: [poor/mediocre/improving]
> - How much that's changing as of tonight: significantly
>
> I'm working on it. You already know.

---

**Note 5 — The Complaint Department**
> A formal grievance:
>
> You have made it very inconvenient to imagine my life without you. I did not ask for this. I had a perfectly functioning life before [how you'd describe meeting them — e.g., "you sat next to me at [place]" / "that first date I almost cancelled" / "you appeared in my life and refused to leave"]. Now look at me.
>
> I'm choosing not to do anything about it. Happy Valentine's Day.

---

**Note 6 — The Simple One (Funny)**
> I like you a truly unreasonable amount. This is your fault entirely.

---

**Note 7 — The Ranking**
> Things I love, ranked:
> 1. You
> 2. [Their favorite food — e.g., "Your cooking" / "The way you make coffee"]
> 3. [Something you share — e.g., "Sunday mornings that go nowhere" / "The show we're currently watching"]
> 4. Everything else (mostly fine)
>
> The ranking has been stable for [length of your relationship]. I don't anticipate any changes.

---

**Note 8 — The Threat**
> I love you so much that I'm choosing to overlook [specific endearing habit — e.g., "the fact that you think [something they're wrong about]" / "that you have never once correctly estimated how long something will take"]. I want you to know that this is love. This is what love looks like.

## SECTION 2: Heartfelt Notes
### For the stops that are meant to land

---

**Note 9 — What You Notice**
> There are things I notice about you that I've never said out loud:
>
> The way you [small specific behavior you love — e.g., "light up when you talk about something you care about" / "get quiet when you're thinking hard" / "always check if I'm okay before you say what you need"]. The way [another specific detail]. The way [a third one].
>
> I notice everything. I just forget to say it. I'm saying it now.

---

**Note 10 — The Before and After**
> There is a version of my life before [how you'd describe meeting them — e.g., "before I knew you" / "before that first night" / "before you"]. That version had fewer good things in it. I don't think about it much. I'm too busy with the version I'm living now.

---

**Note 11 — The Specific Thing**
> My favorite thing about you is [a very specific, particular thing — not "your smile" or "your laugh" but something that only applies to them — e.g., "the way you take something seriously that everyone else thinks is small" / "how you always have an opinion, even when you pretend you don't" / "the particular way you are when it's just us"].
>
> I've never met anyone else who does that in exactly that way. I hope I never stop getting to see it.

---

**Note 12 — What I Want You to Know**
> A few things I want you to know, on this Valentine's Day:
>
> You are loved — not in spite of [something they're self-conscious about], but including it. Not because you're perfect. Because you're [their name or "you"].
>
> I choose you on the easy days and the hard ones. In the version of this life we have now and in all the versions ahead of us. That's not obligation. That's just what I actually want.

---

**Note 13 — The Long Game**
> I am in this for the long version of us. The part after this Valentine's Day. The part after this year. The part that includes [real things in your shared future — e.g., "the apartment we keep talking about" / "watching [their interest or hobby] turn into something more" / "all the ordinary Tuesdays that add up to something"].
>
> I'm not going anywhere. I'm just getting started.

---

**Note 14 — What You've Given Each Other**
> Before you, I didn't know I was looking for [something they brought to your life — e.g., "someone who makes quiet feel like enough" / "a person who challenges me without needing to win" / "this particular kind of home"].
>
> You gave that to me. I don't say it enough.

---

**Note 15 — Simple Heartfelt**
> You make my life better. Not in some abstract way. In the actual day-to-day, specific, measurable way. I am luckier with you than without you, and I know it, and I'm grateful for it every single day.

---

**Note 16 — The Honest One**
> I am not always the easiest person to love. You already know that. The thing is — you do it anyway, in ways I see even when I don't say I do. That means more to me than I've probably made clear.
>
> Tonight is partly an apology for that. And partly a promise to do better at saying it.

## SECTION 3: Memory-Based Notes
### For stops tied to specific shared experiences

---

**Note 17 — The Memory**
> Do you remember [specific memory — e.g., "the night we [specific event]" / "the first time we [specific first]" / "that afternoon at [place]"]?
>
> I think about that [day / night / moment] more than you'd expect. [Add one specific detail you remember — something they might not know you noticed.] I never told you that.
>
> That was a good [day / night / moment]. I want a hundred more like it.

---

**Note 18 — The Accidental Milestone**
> There's a [day / moment / conversation] that I didn't know at the time was important. It was [describe a moment that was ordinary when it happened but now feels significant — e.g., "just a regular [day of week] when we [activity]" / "that one night when nothing particular was planned"].
>
> It was the moment I started being sure. I don't think you knew that then. You probably know it now.

---

**Note 19 — The Trip or Experience**
> [Trip/event name or description — e.g., "That trip to [place]" / "The weekend we [specific experience]"] is one of my top-five memories. Not because everything went perfectly — [acknowledge something that didn't go perfectly if applicable — e.g., "we got lost for two hours" / "the place we booked was nothing like the photos"]. Maybe partly because of that.
>
> I want more of those. The imperfect ones where the best part was just who I was with.

---

**Note 20 — The Small One**
> My favorite memory of us isn't one of the big ones. It's [a specific small moment — e.g., "the Sunday morning when we [specific mundane thing]" / "the time you [small kind thing they did]" / "the five minutes we spent [ordinary thing] that turned into an hour because neither of us wanted to stop"].
>
> The small ones are the ones I keep.

---

**Note 21 — The Thing You Saved**
> I've been saving [a specific memory, feeling, or plan] to tell you. I was going to say it [some time ago] and then I kept waiting for the right moment.
>
> [The thing you've been meaning to say — something genuine, even if small.]
>
> There. Said.

---

**Note 22 — The First Time**
> The first time I [specific early memory in your relationship — e.g., "heard you laugh like that" / "saw how you are with [people they care about]" / "realized you were going to matter to me"], I didn't say anything about it. I just filed it away.
>
> I've been adding to that file ever since. It's full.

## SECTION 4: Future Promises
### For notes that look forward instead of back

---

**Note 23 — The Plan**
> Here is what I want for us:
>
> [List 3–5 specific things you genuinely want for your future together — not generic, but real ones. E.g., "More mornings with nowhere to be" / "That trip to [place you've talked about]" / "Whatever version of [thing you've discussed] turns out to look like" / "More ordinary days that don't seem special but actually are".]
>
> That's the plan. Or at least, that's the beginning of it.

---

**Note 24 — The Promise**
> I promise you:
>
> To [something specific and genuine — e.g., "keep choosing you on the days when choosing anyone would be easier" / "keep showing up, even when showing up is harder than I make it look" / "remember to say out loud the things I think about you silently"].
>
> And to [a second specific promise].
>
> These aren't New Year's resolutions. These are just who I'm trying to be for you.

---

**Note 25 — The Thing I'm Looking Forward To**
> I'm most looking forward to [something specific in your shared near future — e.g., "[a trip you've planned]" / "[an upcoming life event]" / "figuring out [something you're working through together]"]. Not because it'll be easy. Because you'll be there.
>
> Everything's better with you there.

---

**Note 26 — The Ordinary Future**
> I don't need a life full of extraordinary moments. I just want [a specific ordinary version of your future — e.g., "more evenings like the ones we already have" / "the version of us ten years from now doing exactly what we do now" / "someone to split the check of ordinary life with — the good kind of check"].
>
> That future, with you, is enough. It's more than enough. It's everything.

---

**Note 27 — The Version of You I'll Get to Know**
> I'm excited about the versions of you I haven't met yet. The one ten years from now who's figured out [something they're working on]. The one who's been through more and come out more sure of themselves. The one who still [something enduring about them that you love].
>
> I want to know all of them. I plan to.

---

**Note 28 — The Long Note**
> Here's what I think about when I think about our future:
>
> I think about how we're going to handle things we haven't faced yet. And I think: I want to face them with you. Not because you're perfect or because I think it'll always be easy — but because [something true about why you trust them in the hard times — e.g., "you're honest even when it's uncomfortable" / "you care about getting it right more than being right" / "I know you'll still be choosing us even when it's hard"].
>
> That's the person I want in my corner. That's you.
>
> Happy Valentine's Day. Here's to everything we haven't gotten to yet.

---

**Note 29 — Short and Forward**
> I'm not sure where we're going. But I'm glad we're going there together. Happy Valentine's Day.

---

**Note 30 — The Last One**
> You are one of the great decisions of my life. Not the loudest one. Just one of the most sure.
>
> I love you. Happy Valentine's Day.
>
> — [Your name / "the person who planned all of this"]

## How to Use These Notes

**During the scavenger hunt:**
Select 4–6 notes and place one at each clue location alongside the clue card. Vary the tone — place a funny note early in the hunt, a heartfelt one at the heavy stop, a memory-based one at the location connected to that memory, and a future promise at the final reveal.

**As standalone Valentine's Day notes:**
Any of these work as a standalone card or letter. The heartfelt section (Notes 9–16) and future promises (Notes 23–30) are particularly strong as standalone pieces.

**Personalizing them:**
The notes are written to be personalized with specific details. The more specific your fill-ins, the more powerful the note. "You make my life better" is nice. "You make my life better — specifically the part where [specific thing]" is the kind of note that gets kept.

**Printing tips:**
Print on cardstock or use a slightly nicer paper than plain white printer paper. A small effort on the presentation makes the words land harder. Hand-writing them, if your handwriting is legible, is even better than printing.
